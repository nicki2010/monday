Depths of the Sea

I chose to draw this image because it represents how women are still suffering due to the eyes of society when it comes to gender roles. It shows how women are drowning in missed opportunities, norms when it comes to gender roles, and their misconception within the society. Whereas up until now, even if eras changed, the conception about women being weak and incompetent enough to not do the things men can do are still within the society today. The image shows how the girl still tried to reach up to the surface—the light in fact—as a sign of hope in this cruel world. Yet with desperation to reach the light, the weight of the sea would still drown her as the sea represents the weight of the misconception of the society to women, the missed opportunities of women to change the world for the better, to prove themselves that they're worth more than what society sees them. Yet with nothing to do about the society's misconceptions, she's left to gaze at the light of hope while drowning in the depths of the sea.

- Kate Paula G. Boncales </span> </div>
